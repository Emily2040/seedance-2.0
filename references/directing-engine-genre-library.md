# Directing Engine — Genre Worked-Example Library

On-demand companion to [`directing-engine.md`](directing-engine.md). Load this only when you want a fully worked example in a specific genre.

The canonical lane criteria and records live in [`directors-read.md`](directors-read.md); the Coherence Principle, Director's Voice, and long-form spine live in the engine. These entries apply both layers. Every narrative entry writes all ten canonical fields, while every utility, product-only, process, or ambient entry uses only the two-field non-narrative record. Each then runs the same read -> intention -> voice -> coherent setup -> performance -> compiled-prompt procedure, and the contrast across genres is the lesson: the voice and instruments change because the scene is doing something different.

## Worked Examples by Genre

Each example runs the same procedure: read, intention, voice, coherent setup, performance, and one compiled prompt sentence. Notice that the voice and instruments change because the scene is doing something different every time. Keep faces, hands, logos, and text stable in every prompt.

### Product / commercial reveal

- **Idea:** "a luxury watch ad."
- **Read - non-narrative lane.** `utility intent`: prove the watch's brushed-steel case, dial text, and second-hand movement remain precise through a silhouette-to-hero reveal; `non-narrative refusal`: no invented wearer, desire, rivalry, seduction, status psychology, or dramatic power shift
- **Intention:** make the audience covet the craftsmanship. **Voice:** composed classicist.
- **Setup:** locked slider from a raking silhouette to a front three-quarter hero ending on the dial; gentle compression; one narrow warm key sweeping the brushed case with controlled speculars on black; clean room tone with a single mechanical tick.
- **Performance:** the watch is the performer - the second hand completes one precise sweep.
- **Prompt:** `@Image1 is the watch; preserve case, dial, hands, and logo exactly. Slow slider from a raking silhouette to a front three-quarter hero ending on the dial; gentle compression; one narrow warm key sweeps the brushed steel with controlled speculars on a black background; the second hand completes a single smooth sweep; low room tone with one soft mechanical tick; do not alter case shape, dial text, or proportions.`

### Music video beat

- **Idea:** "a singer in a neon room for a synth track."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: release the synth build on one earned downbeat; `turn`: held restraint to one sharp physical release; `POV`: the singer caught inside the rhythm; `power shift`: the singer holds the frame until the downbeat lets music and light take control; `hidden want/objective`: contain the body's impulse until the track authorizes release; `obstacle/tactic`: the rising synth keeps delaying the payoff, so the singer locks both shoulders square through the build; `subtext/contradiction`: severe self-control makes the eventual euphoria credible; `visible suppressed behavior`: both shoulders stay square until one sharp head-and-shoulder snap lands on the downbeat; `non-transferable detail`: the left magenta tube beside the microphone blinks once a beat late; `stock solution refused`: no rapid-cut performance montage, free-floating neon haze, or motion on every beat
- **Intention:** make the body feel the downbeat hit. **Voice:** expressive stylist.
- **Setup:** a designed move that snaps from a slow push to a held wide on the drop; bold magenta and cyan from visible practical tubes; saturated signature palette; the cut and a light pulse lock to the beat; gestural performance.
- **Performance:** the artist holds stillness through the build, then one sharp gesture lands exactly on the downbeat as the lights pulse.
- **Prompt:** `Character A at a microphone in a neon room lit by visible magenta and cyan tubes; both shoulders stay square through the build, then one sharp head-and-shoulder snap lands on the downbeat as the camera changes from a slow push to a held wide; the left magenta tube beside the microphone blinks once a beat late; saturated high-contrast palette; keep the face stable and put motion only on the release.`

### Horror / suspense

- **Idea:** "someone hears a noise in the basement."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: build dread without spending the unseen threat; `turn`: protected landing to trapped threshold; `POV`: the listener at the top of the basement stairs; `power shift`: she owns the lit landing until the off-screen scrape takes control of the stairwell; `hidden want/objective`: identify the sound without stepping below the bulb; `obstacle/tactic`: darkness hides the source, so she stops breathing and listens through the rail; `subtext/contradiction`: her searching gaze advances while the rest of her body wants to retreat; `visible suppressed behavior`: her thumb rubs the chipped green paint on the rail once and then stops; `non-transferable detail`: chipped green paint on the rail casts a thin shadow across the third stair; `stock solution refused`: no creature reveal, jump cut, reaction close-up, or score sting
- **Intention:** make the audience afraid of what they cannot see. **Voice:** intimate minimalist tilting to graphic.
- **Setup:** a very slow creeping push on a doorway that keeps the danger unseen; high-ratio low-key with one bare practical and deep pooling shadow; sound thickens with one off-screen cue inside held silence; no jump, only withholding.
- **Performance:** she stops, holds her breath, hand on the rail, eyes searching the dark - the one true gesture is the breath she does not take.
- **Prompt:** `Medium shot of Character A at the top of a basement stairwell, one bare bulb throwing hard shadow; she stops, holds her breath, and rubs the chipped green paint on the rail once with her thumb before going still, eyes searching the dark below; the rail casts a thin shadow across the third stair; very slow push that keeps the bottom unseen; near-silence with one low off-screen scrape; no on-screen reveal; keep the face stable.`

### Anime / 2D power-up

- **Idea:** "an original anime hero gathers resolve before a fight."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: convert doubt into controlled commitment before the fight; `turn`: coiled hesitation to a planted stance; `POV`: the hero reading the opponent from inside the pause; `power shift`: the implied opponent owns the wait until the hero closes a fist and reclaims the frame; `hidden want/objective`: stand ground without exposing fear; `obstacle/tactic`: a loose wrist wrap betrays the tremor, so the hero reties it before moving; `subtext/contradiction`: repair of the costume carries the vow that dialogue would overstate; `visible suppressed behavior`: two fingers retie the loose knot once, pause, and only then close into a fist; `non-transferable detail`: a torn red wrist wrap has one visible white repair stitch; `stock solution refused`: no screaming transformation, expanding energy sphere, orbiting rubble, or photographic lens move
- **Intention:** make the audience feel the charge of resolve. **Voice:** expressive stylist in 2D grammar.
- **Setup:** no photographic lens language; a held tense frame then a burst of radiating motion lines and one impact frame on the power-up; flat but dramatic cel light with a hard rim; saturated key color; one held beat, then a hard snap. Load [2d-anime-grammar](2d-anime-grammar.md).
- **Performance:** the body holds coiled, then a single explosive stance change on the burst frame.
- **Prompt:** `Original 2D cel-animated hero with a torn red wrist wrap and one white repair stitch; in a held coiled frame, two fingers retie the loose knot once, pause, and close into a fist before one explosive stance change with radiating motion lines and a single impact frame; saturated key color, flat cel lighting, hard rim light, no photographic lens language; keep the character design and silhouette consistent.`

### Action / pursuit

- **Idea:** "a courier runs from someone through a market."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: narrow the courier's escape choices while keeping the route legible; `turn`: open market lane to near entrapment; `POV`: the runner measuring the next obstacle over one shoulder; `power shift`: the courier owns the gap until the pursuer enters the same stall lane; `hidden want/objective`: reach the blue awning before the pursuer can cut off the exit; `obstacle/tactic`: a fruit stall blocks the line, so the courier plants one palm and redirects around it; `subtext/contradiction`: protecting the delivery matters more than looking back at the threat; `visible suppressed behavior`: the courier clamps the torn satchel strap to the chest instead of turning to check the pursuer; `non-transferable detail`: green limes roll away from a dented brass market scale; `stock solution refused`: no speed ramp, impossible whip pan, random collision montage, or changing screen direction
- **Intention:** make the audience feel the closing distance. **Voice:** kinetic visceral.
- **Setup:** handheld tracking at the runner's shoulder; strict screen direction; hard high-contrast daylight between stalls; sound thickens to breath, footfalls, and clatter; one clear physical objective.
- **Performance:** effort and consequence - a shove off a stall, a stumble and recovery.
- **Prompt:** `Handheld tracking at Character A's shoulder as he runs left to right through a crowded market, clamping a torn satchel strap to his chest instead of looking back; he plants one palm on a fruit stall and recovers as green limes roll from a dented brass scale while the pursuer closes in the same lane; hard daylight between stalls; breath, footfalls, and market clatter; keep screen direction consistent and the face stable enough to read effort.`

### Comedy pratfall

- **Idea:** "a confident man walks into a glass door."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: puncture self-importance in one dry, observable mistake; `turn`: composed entrance to exposed embarrassment; `POV`: the lobby's static witness; `power shift`: the man controls the stride until the glass and held frame get the last word; `hidden want/objective`: pass reception without acknowledging the collision; `obstacle/tactic`: the glass stops him, so he repairs his appearance and continues; `subtext/contradiction`: the careful recovery insists nothing happened while proving that it did; `visible suppressed behavior`: his palm stops short of his sore nose and redirects to straighten his collar; `non-transferable detail`: a crooked red VISITOR sticker stays folded on his left lapel; `stock solution refused`: no reaction zoom, cutaway witness, canned laugh, or music cue
- **Intention:** land the timing of the pratfall. **Voice:** graphic formalist, deadpan.
- **Setup:** a locked symmetrical wide with clean geography so the glass is fair but invisible; a deadpan hold past comfort; one absurd visible beat; a single dry SFX; no camera reaction.
- **Performance:** full commitment - confident stride, the smack, a beat of stillness, then he straightens his collar and continues.
- **Prompt:** `Locked symmetrical wide of a clean glass-fronted lobby; Character A, wearing a crooked red VISITOR sticker folded on his left lapel, strides into the glass door with one dry thud and holds; his palm stops short of his nose and redirects to straighten his collar before he continues; flat even light, no camera movement or reaction zoom; hold the frame past comfort.`

### UGC / caught real moment

- **Idea:** "a phone clip of a dog stealing a sandwich."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: catch the theft before the phone holder can intervene; `turn`: quiet lunch setup to a failed kitchen chase; `POV`: the late-reacting person behind the phone; `power shift`: the filmer owns the frame until the dog seizes the sandwich and exits; `hidden want/objective`: snatch the sandwich and escape before the reaching hand arrives; `obstacle/tactic`: the counter edge and dish towel reduce traction, so the dog crouches before one committed lunge; `subtext/contradiction`: the half-second-late rescue attempt becomes the joke; `visible suppressed behavior`: one front paw slides on the blue dish towel, catches, and launches without a second attempt; `non-transferable detail`: the sandwich is wrapped in a green checked napkin with one corner trailing; `stock solution refused`: no stabilized chase, slow motion, reaction insert, or added music
- **Intention:** make it feel genuinely caught, not staged. **Voice:** observational naturalist with a phone-UGC source lock.
- **Setup:** vertical handheld phone footage, slight grip sway, an auto-exposure shift on the pan, ambient kitchen sound; embrace the artifacts, because faux-clean reads fake. Load the source-look lock in [seedance-style](../skills/seedance-style/SKILL.md).
- **Performance:** the dog's one decisive lunge and grab; a human hand reaches a half-second too late.
- **Prompt:** `Vertical handheld phone footage with slight grip sway and an auto-exposure shift; a dog on the counter crouches, one front paw slides on a blue dish towel and catches, then it lunges once, grabs a sandwich wrapped in a green checked napkin with one corner trailing, and bolts as an off-screen hand reaches a half-second too late; kitchen room tone, no music; keep the grab the single beat.`

### Transformation / VFX brand reveal

- **Idea:** "frost spreads across a window and stops at a logo."
- **Read - non-narrative lane.** `utility intent`: show frost crystals advancing from the glass edge while the etched logo remains clear, exact, and legible at the endpoint; `non-narrative refusal`: no invented sentient cold, brand rivalry, seduction, character objective, or emotional reveal
- **Intention:** make the transformation feel inevitable and exact. **Voice:** composed classicist.
- **Setup:** a locked macro so the change is fully legible; cool motivated side light that rakes the crystal growth; one cause - cold - with visible consequences; a clean bed with a faint crackle; ends on the protected logo.
- **Performance:** the material is the performer - crystals advance and halt cleanly at the logo edge.
- **Prompt:** `Locked macro on a clear glass pane; over the clip, frost crystals grow inward from the edges and halt cleanly at the etched logo, leaving it clear; cool motivated side light rakes the crystal growth; one cause, cold, with visible crystal advance; quiet bed with a faint ice crackle; preserve the logo shape and keep the camera locked.`

### Establishing / landscape

### Food / culinary

### Automotive / commercial

### High fashion / editorial

- **Idea:** "an editorial frame of a model in a sculptural coat."

### Talking-head pitch (口播)

- **Idea:** "a founder pitches a product to camera."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: earn enough trust for one concrete next step; `turn`: viewer skepticism to provisional willingness to try; `POV`: the viewer addressed across the lens; `power shift`: the viewer withholds belief until the founder points to physical proof; `hidden want/objective`: secure a trial without sounding as if the result is guaranteed; `obstacle/tactic`: the benefit sounds abstract, so the founder redirects the claim toward the prototype; `subtext/contradiction`: the restrained gesture admits that proof, not charisma, must carry the pitch; `visible suppressed behavior`: the thumb taps the prototype's chipped blue corner once before the hand opens toward it; `non-transferable detail`: a scratched palm-sized prototype has one chipped blue corner; `stock solution refused`: no frantic jump cuts, floating interface graphics, magic transformation, or repeated call-to-action
- **Intention:** make the audience trust and act. **Voice:** composed classicist, caption-safe.
- **Setup:** locked medium close-up; soft even key; a tidy on-brand background; minimal head motion; caption-safe headroom; clean room tone; short quoted lines.
- **Performance:** steady eye contact, one open-hand gesture on the key point, restrained.
- **Prompt:** `Locked medium close-up of an original founder addressing camera with a scratched palm-sized prototype on the desk, one blue corner chipped; soft even key with caption-safe headroom; steady eye contact as the thumb taps that chipped blue corner once, then one open-hand gesture directs the viewer back to the prototype; minimal head motion and clean room tone; keep framing stable for captions.`

### Short drama (短剧)

- **Idea:** "she reads the text that changes everything."

### Romance

- **Idea:** "two people almost hold hands on a bench."
- **Read - narrative lane.** `dramatic function`: stage mutual risk without completing the touch; `turn`: guarded parallel stillness to a shared almost-choice; `POV`: the gap between both resting hands; `power shift`: neither person leads until one pinky moves and the other closes half the distance; `hidden want/objective`: test whether contact is welcome without forcing an answer; `obstacle/tactic`: either movement can be misread, so both keep their bodies still and let fingers negotiate; `subtext/contradiction`: the smaller the gesture, the larger the unspoken request; `visible suppressed behavior`: one thumb rubs an orange thread knot at the wrist, stops, and releases the pinky toward the gap; `non-transferable detail`: a short orange thread is tied with a double knot around the left wrist; `stock solution refused`: no kiss, swelling score, orbiting camera, dialogue confession, or lens-flare climax
